Monthly budget

A limit in US dollars per agent and calendar month. At 80% the agent is marked; at 100% it is paused: no panel chat or new tasks, and its waiting tasks are put on hold. Work already running is not cut off. The pause ends on the 1st of next month or when you raise the limit. Leave empty for no limit.
